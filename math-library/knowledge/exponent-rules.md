# Exponent Rules (Quick Reference)

These rules apply where the expressions are defined. Unless noted otherwise, assume the base is nonzero.

---

## 1. Negative Exponents (Reciprocals)

A negative exponent means **take the reciprocal**.

**Rule**

- $a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^n}$

**Examples**

- $3^{-4}=\dfrac{1}{81}$
- $(-3)^{-4}=\dfrac{1}{81}$

---

## 2. Parentheses vs. No Parentheses

Parentheses determine whether the negative sign is part of the base.

**Rule**

- $(-a)^n$ and $-a^n$ are not generally the same expression.

**Examples**

- $(-3)^{-4}=\dfrac{1}{81}$
- $-3^{-4}=-(3^{-4})=-\dfrac{1}{81}$

---

## 3. Even vs. Odd Powers

- Even powers of a negative base produce a **positive** result.
- Odd powers of a negative base keep the **negative** sign.

**Examples**

- $(-2)^4=16$
- $(-2)^3=-8$

---

## 4. Product Rule (Same Base)

**Rule**

- $a^m\cdot a^n=a^{m+n}$

**Example**

- $3^2\cdot 3^3=3^5$

---

## 5. Quotient Rule (Same Base)

**Rule**

- $\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}$

**Example**

- $\dfrac{3^5}{3^2}=3^3$

---

## 6. Power of a Power

**Rule**

- $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$

**Example**

- $(3^2)^4=3^8$

---

## 7. Power of a Quotient

**Rule**

- $\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^n=\dfrac{a^n}{b^n}$, where $b\ne 0$.

**Example**

- $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{9}{4}$

---

## 8. Rational Exponent Rule

**Rule**

- $a^{m/n}=\left(\sqrt[n]{a}\right)^m=\sqrt[n]{a^m}$

For real-valued expressions, an even root requires a nonnegative radicand.

**Example**

- $16^{3/4}=\left(\sqrt[4]{16}\right)^3=2^3=8$

---

## 9. Zero Exponent

**Rule**

- $a^0=1$, where $a\ne 0$.

**Example**

- $5^0=1$
