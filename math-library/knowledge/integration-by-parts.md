# Integration by Parts

## Formula
$\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du$

Product rule → integration by parts

### When to Consider Integration by Parts
Products such as:
- $x e^x$
- $x \cos x$
- $x \ln x$
...

### Choosing u: LIPET
L — Logarithmic     
I — Inverse trigonometric   
P — Power of x  
E — Exponential     
T — Trigonometric   

### Important Classification
$x^n$ → Power of x      
$e^x$ → Exponential

## Procedure
1. Identify possible u and dv.
2. Use LIPET to choose u.
3. Differentiate u → du.
4. Integrate dv → v.
5. Substitute into ∫u dv = uv - ∫v du.
6. Simplify.
7. Add +C.
