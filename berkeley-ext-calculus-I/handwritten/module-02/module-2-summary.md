A limit exists only if both one-sided limits exist and are equal.

## Limit Laws (1–10)

### 1. Sum Law
\[
\lim_{x \to a}[f(x)+g(x)] = \lim_{x \to a} f(x) + \lim_{x \to a} g(x)
\]

---

### 2. Difference Law
\[
\lim_{x \to a}[f(x)-g(x)] = \lim_{x \to a} f(x) - \lim_{x \to a} g(x)
\]

---

### 3. Constant Multiple Law
\[
\lim_{x \to a}[c\,f(x)] = c \lim_{x \to a} f(x)
\]

---

### 4. Product Law
\[
\lim_{x \to a}[f(x)g(x)] = \lim_{x \to a} f(x)\cdot \lim_{x \to a} g(x)
\]

---

### 5. Quotient Law
\[
\lim_{x \to a}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \frac{\lim_{x \to a} f(x)}{\lim_{x \to a} g(x)},
\quad \text{if } \lim_{x \to a} g(x) \neq 0
\]

---

### 6. Power Law
\[
\lim_{x \to a}[f(x)]^n = \left(\lim_{x \to a} f(x)\right)^n,
\quad n \in \mathbb{Z}^+
\]

---

### 7. Root Law
\[
\lim_{x \to a}\sqrt[n]{f(x)} = \sqrt[n]{\lim_{x \to a} f(x)},
\quad n \in \mathbb{Z}^+
\]

*(If \(n\) is even, assume \(\lim_{x \to a} f(x) > 0\).)*

---

### 8. Constant Law
\[
\lim_{x \to a} c = c
\]

---

### 9. Identity Law
\[
\lim_{x \to a} x = a
\]

---

### 10. Special Power Limit
\[
\lim_{x \to a} x^n = a^n,
\quad n \in \mathbb{Z}^+
\]
