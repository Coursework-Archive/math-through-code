1. Simplify the integrand if possible 
    - Expand powers if necessary. 
    - Rewrite radicals as fractional exponents
    - Factor constants out.

2. Look for basic antiderivative
Ask:
* Is it a polynomial?
$$ \int x^ndx $$
* Is it exponential?
$$ \int e^x dx $$
$$ \int a^x dx $$
* Is it logarithmic?
$$ \int \frac{1}{x} dx $$
* Is it trigonometric?
$$ \int \sin x  \,dx $$
$$ \int \cos x  \,dx $$
$$ \int \sec^2 x  \,dx $$

3. Look for a composition (inside function)

Example:

$$ \int \frac{z^2}{z^2 + 1} dz $$

$$ u = z^2 + 1 $$

$$ du = 3x^2dz $$
