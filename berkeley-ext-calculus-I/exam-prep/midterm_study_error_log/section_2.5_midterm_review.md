#### Fix Rule:
Continuity is always stated at a point (x = a), and requires limit exists and equals f(a)
The function is discontinuous at:
x = -4, -2, 2 (holes)
x = 4 (vertical asymptote)

The function is continuous at all other points in its domain.

In particular:
- f is continuous at x = 2
- f is not continuous at x = 4 because the limit does not exist
At x = 4:

The left-hand limit does not exist ⇒ not continuous from the left

The right-hand limit equals f(4) ⇒ continuous from the right
